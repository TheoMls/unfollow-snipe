Analyze Your Instagram Followers Privately

Unfollow Snipe is a privacy-focused tool that helps you identify Instagram users who don't follow you back. Unlike other apps, Unfollow Snipe runs 100% inside your browser using your exported Data Download files. You never have to log in with your account or grant access to third-party APIs.

How to Export Your Instagram Data

Follow these quick steps to download your official JSON files safely from Instagram.

  1. 1 Request Your Data Export

    Open Instagram and navigate to:
    Accounts Center → Your Information and Permissions → Export your information → Create Export → Export to device.

  2. 2 Configure Export Settings
    • Information: Select Customize information and check only Followers and following.
    • Date Range: Change to All time.
    • Format: Must be set to JSON (HTML format will not work).
    • Media Quality: Low or Medium (Media quality does not affect JSON data speed).
  3. 3 Extract & Upload

    Once Instagram emails your download link, unzip the downloaded .zip file and navigate to:

    connections/followers_and_following/

    Select and upload your followers_1.json and following.json files below.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my Instagram account safe? ▼

Yes. Traditional unfollower apps ask for your Instagram password, which risks account bans or compromise. Unfollow Snipe processes your official data export locally in JavaScript—your login credentials and files are never seen or stored by anyone.

Does Unfollow Snipe store my uploaded files? ▼

No. All parsing happens entirely within your web browser's temporary memory. Refreshing or closing the tab completely clears all data.

Can using Unfollow Snipe get my Instagram account banned or flagged? ▼

No. Instagram bans accounts that use third-party tools requiring login credentials or automated scrapers. Because Unfollow Snipe only reads static files on your device and makes zero network requests to Instagram's servers, it is impossible for Instagram to detect or restrict your account.

How long does it take for Instagram to send my data export file? ▼

Instagram usually generates and emails your download link within 5 to 15 minutes. For older or highly active accounts with large histories, it can occasionally take up to a few hours.

Does Unfollow Snipe work on mobile devices? ▼

Yes. You can request your data download in the Instagram mobile app, save the JSON files to your iPhone (Files app) or Android device (Downloads folder), and upload them directly to unfollowsnipe.com in your mobile web browser.

Why do some profile links show "Page not available" when I click them? ▼

If a user has deactivated their account, changed their username, or blocked you after your data export was generated, Instagram's web link for that old handle will direct to a broken page.

Is Unfollow Snipe affiliated with Instagram or Meta? ▼

Unfollow Snipe is an independent, free open-source privacy utility and is not affiliated with, authorized by, or endorsed by Instagram or Meta Platforms, Inc.
